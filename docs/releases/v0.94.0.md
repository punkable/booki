# Booki 0.94.0

## Groups that are easier to use

- Drop files and folders from Explorer directly into an open group, without pinning them on the dock first. The group stays open and retains its scroll position.
- Scroll long groups internally with a fixed header. The four-icon dock thumbnail is a preview, not a membership limit. Drag near the content edges to scroll while reordering.
- Take members out with dragging or their context menu, including Shift+F10 / the menu key. Corner extraction buttons are removed.
- Click the group’s header icon to choose its symbol and a colour from Booki’s Pantone-inspired screen palette. Appearance edits preserve the other dock widgets and resolve the current saved group after a rename.
- Native-glass flyouts use translation without an opacity fade and report their geometry during opening, avoiding the deliberate wait until the fade ends before applying native material.

## Device readings, AI and media

- Visible device batteries refresh every 15 seconds, with slower polling in energy-saving mode. A Refresh action updates both the device panel and dock; Bluetooth settings are available beside it.
- An unavailable device shows `?` and a clear explanation rather than retaining its old battery level indefinitely. Faster querying cannot make Windows or HID++ produce a newer hardware reading.
- AI cards rotate through detected providers, including idle ones, with their local logos and colours. Codex shows the remaining percentage of its most restrictive reported quota window. Claude shows local token counts and unknown quota when logs do not include a plan allowance. No credential or network access is added.
- A paused media card retires after one minute and becomes available again when playback resumes. This does not introduce per-monitor identification of the media player.

## Collector, notices and documentation

- Delayed progress events no longer overwrite a completed or failed Collector result. Failure details are available on the card.
- Update announcements wait during focus, Do not disturb, video or games. Download and installation remain explicit, separate actions; settings are saved and backed up before applying.
- Both READMEs use composed captures of the real frontend with offline example icons and data. Recent release history is generated from the changelog. Capture fingerprints also cover layout, fixtures and typography.

Automated browser coverage uses a simulated Windows bridge. Windows CI checks native regressions and packages both architectures; neither browser tests nor packaging demonstrates physical MX Keys Mini charging, native desktop composition, multi-monitor interaction or an installed upgrade on a person's PC. These hardware/runtime limits remain explicit.
