# Booki 0.85.0

A rebuilt look for the dock: four styles that differ at a glance, real blurred glass, and colors that say what they change.

- **Four styles.** Taskbar, Glass, Clear glass and Solid. Each card in Settings is painted with its real look, and the dock's right-click menu switches between them in one click.
- **Fill apart from the accent.** The dock's fill (automatic, wallpaper, accent or a picked color) and the accent color (yours, Windows' or the wallpaper's) are separate settings, so choosing "Like Windows" for the accent no longer pretends to change the dock. The Taskbar style paints the Windows taskbar's own color and follows the Windows mode.
- **New glass.** On Windows 11 the blur comes from Windows' composition backdrop, clipped to the dock's rounded shape, so the rectangle that showed inside the dock is gone. The gloss and reflections were removed. If the composition backdrop is unavailable, Booki falls back to the previous blur.
- **A fuller right-click menu.** Right-clicking the dock or the notch opens the dock's full menu: add, search, App library, the style row, Arrange, Appearance and Settings.
- **The notch hides whole.** It no longer stays half visible, cut at the edge, after it steps away from a maximized app.
- Your existing colors and finish are converted to the new settings on first launch.

## Verification limits

Automated browser tests use a simulated Windows bridge, and the Windows CI builds and packages the app. The composition backdrop, the notch-opened menu and the Taskbar match need to be checked on a Windows 11 device.
