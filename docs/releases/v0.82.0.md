# Booki 0.82.0

The dock goes to the window you already have open, the notch steps aside while you work, and groups form by dragging.

- **Open apps, not new copies.** Clicking an app that is already open goes to its window; with several windows a picker lists them, plus "New window". Clicking the app that is already in front minimizes it, like the taskbar. The icon pops while an app is starting.
- **Quick launcher.** Ctrl+Alt+Space (configurable in Settings > System > Shortcuts) searches pinned items, installed apps and folders, or switches to an open window.
- **Notch that gets out of the way.** By default the notch hides while a maximized or fullscreen app is in front and comes back when the pointer nears it, a new song starts or a focus timer ends. Turn off "Move the notch aside while you work" in Settings > Dock to keep it on screen. The Island also flashes the volume level and says when a focus block ends.
- **Easier groups.** On the dock, dropping an app anywhere across the middle of another makes or joins a group, and a member dragged out of a group's panel lands where you drop it. In Settings > Apps you can drag apps into a group and back out, and the group panel shows its members.
- **Weather and panels.** A weather forecast, and redesigned panels for weather, focus, tasks, calendar and notes.
- **Automatic profiles.** A profile can switch on its own with an external monitor or on a schedule.
- **Clearer Settings.** Plain names for the notch options (shape: Tab, Pill or Dot), labelled Position controls, Profiles and Updates aligned with the rest, Home shows Booki's own memory and CPU use, and pins without an icon get a coloured initial.

## Verification limits

Automated browser tests use a simulated Windows bridge. Hiding the notch over maximized apps, the window picker and the launcher need to be checked on a Windows 11 device.
