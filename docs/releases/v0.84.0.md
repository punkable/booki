# Booki 0.84.0

Booki behaves more like the Windows taskbar, the dock takes the taskbar's color in every finish, and a full review of the dock, Settings and the native core fixed a long list of bugs.

## Like the taskbar

- **Keyboard.** The dock is one Tab stop. Arrow keys, Home and End move between icons, and a screen reader announces "Running · N windows".
- **Clicks.** Shift+click or middle-click opens another window, Ctrl+Shift+click runs as administrator (also in the menu), and Ctrl+middle-click opens the file location.
- **Attention.** When an app flashes on the taskbar, its icon in the dock is marked until you open it.
- **Notifications.** Windows notifications when a focus timer ends, even with the dock hidden, and the first time in a session an update is found.
- **Recent files.** A pinned app's menu lists the files from its Windows Jump List alongside those opened through Booki.
- **High contrast, text size and power.** Dock, notch, menus and Settings use the system colors in high contrast. Settings follows the Windows text size. With the screen off, polling stops; with energy saver on, it slows down. Changing a monitor's scale re-places the dock.
- **Language.** The tray menu and the Settings title follow Booki's language.

## Colors and groups

- The dock fill follows the taskbar or the wallpaper in Glass, Mica and Solid, and dark glass no longer shows a square behind the bar.
- In Settings > Apps, groups and profiles open in place instead of in a panel below.

## Fixes

- **Reset to defaults** saved an old settings revision, so the next launch re-ran every migration and switched finish, notch shape and "focus if running" back. Reset now also re-applies the default shortcuts and leaves Start with Windows as it is.
- **Console programs** (cmd, PowerShell, command-line tools) pinned to the dock started hidden. They open with their window now, in their own folder.
- **Clipboard history** with favorites moved an old favorite to the top once the list was full. The newest copy stays first.
- **Smart hide** measured the whole dock window, flyout room included, after a reveal, so a window near the bar could tuck it away.
- **Renaming a profile** only by capitalization ("work" to "Work") always failed.
- **Automatic profiles** started with an empty schedule that never matched, and their menus missed profiles duplicated or renamed meanwhile.
- **Settings > Apps:** the Undo button vanished right after adding something, two quick additions could show a false "changed in another window" conflict, a finished drag could pull an app out of its group on the next catalog drag, a group could open after the pointer had left it, `localhost:3000`-style addresses were rejected, and the app catalog jumped back to page 1 when the window regained focus.
- **Settings search** for an option hidden by another setting now goes to the control that shows it.
- **Dock:** arrow keys got stuck on a widget hidden while unavailable; the clipboard flyout could reopen after being closed; "Open" on a subfolder's menu closed the folder flyout; a group drag released over nothing moved the item to the end; cancelling a long press still entered edit mode; a group's color leaked into the clipboard, add and launcher flyouts; leaving fullscreen could show the dock over another fullscreen app; a failed save could keep the dock pinned open; a right-click on the notch also revealed the dock; folders dropped onto a group became apps; and widgets offered a Rename that did nothing.

## Verification limits

Automated browser tests use a simulated Windows bridge, and the Windows CI builds and packages the app. None of this was run on a Windows 11 device; the keyboard and click combinations, notifications, Jump List recents, high contrast, console apps and smart hide are worth checking there.
