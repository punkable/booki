# Booki 0.88.0

Includes 0.87.1.

## Widgets
- **Updates** (winget, new): the card counts the apps that have an update, and the panel updates one or all of them. winget's table is read by column, so it works in any Windows language.
- **Cloud** (rclone, new): used and total space of every remote, read-only.
- **Lighting** (OpenRGB, new): apply a saved profile or turn the lights off.
- Gauge widgets at Wide and Extra wide sizes show their name beside the ring.
- Notices never cover the dock. They appear on the notch while it is tucked away, or beside the bar while it is out. They cover a finished download, a device crossing 10% (once), and new updates.
- **Collector** (new): drop a link on the dock to save video or audio only from hundreds of sites, image galleries, or plain files. Booki picks the tool from the link. Direct files are fetched by Booki itself. Galleries go to gallery-dl. Anything else goes to yt-dlp, which asks Video or Audio only. One plugin installs yt-dlp, FFmpeg (MP3, up to 4K merges) and gallery-dl together.
- **Devices** rotates through every connected device with an icon for its kind (headset, mouse, keyboard, gamepad), read from the Bluetooth class, Logitech HID++ or the name. One color scale shows the battery: green, amber, red. At 10% the ring pulses, and charging shows a sheen. The panel is cards without plugin jargon.
- **AI usage** shows Claude and ChatGPT in turn with their official logos. Each bar says when its window resets. Claude's sign-in honors `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR`, and an idle window reads as 0%.
- Device and AI panels reuse the last reading instead of showing Loading.
- A click on Media plays or pauses, and a click on Volume mutes. Their panels open from the context menu (Controls).

## Dock and Settings
- Position is picked by clicking a drawing of your screen; monitors are listed by number, resolution and main screen.
- Folder flyouts put Add in the header; names wrap to two lines instead of overlapping.
- The Dock page preview scrolls with the page; Settings gets Mica back.
- Native blur follows surfaces in motion, the notch-to-dock morph included, instead of blinking off.

## Plugins and trust
- Every plugin download is pinned to a SHA-256 shipped in Booki's source.
- [Terms of use](../../TERMS.md) are accepted once before anything third-party installs.
- Plugin errors appear in your language.

## Fixes
- **Fullscreen and monitors:**
  - Fullscreen and maximized detection only count the monitor the dock or notch is on.
  - Booki is never raised over a fullscreen game.
  - Edge gaps use the target monitor's scale.
  - Blur never shows as a ghost at the dock's old spot.
- **Data safety:**
  - A clipboard history that can't be decrypted is kept rather than erased.
  - Deleted clipboard items can't come back.
  - Profile switches no longer push out pre-update backups.
  - Imports keep this PC's automatic profile rules.
- **Dock edits:**
  - A config reload can no longer undo an edit that was being saved.
  - A reload cancels a drag in flight.
  - Undo puts a removed pin back next to its old neighbour.
- **Panels and keyboard:**
  - The note editor closes even if its widget is gone.
  - Clipboard editing doesn't re-copy.
  - Escape on a task rename only cancels the rename.
  - Closing a panel returns focus to its tile.
- **Updates and winget:**
  - Updates reads winget correctly when nothing needs updating.
  - "Set up clouds" no longer goes through a shell.
  - Failed update buttons restore their label.
- **Mixer:**
  - Per-app mute is a speaker button.
  - The volume rail is a thin Fluent rail in both themes.
- **Translations:** a review pass in all five languages.
- Direct downloads cannot hang the queue, and they never overwrite an existing file.
- Timed-out tools end their whole process tree.
- Config saves run off the UI thread.
- A file drop tucks the dock again.
- Group drops are kept only once saved.
- Stale hide timers no longer cut the morph short.
- Removing a running plugin asks you to close it first.

Browser regressions cover the new flows. Device batteries, AI endpoints, Mica, downloads and the morph still need a physical Windows PC.
