# Booki 1.0.2

A notch that keeps you company on any monitor, and simpler settings.

## Notch

- **Any monitor.** A video or game on another monitor no longer silences the main notch; music and agents keep showing.
- **Music.** Choose Brief (a few seconds per song) or While playing.
- **Claude desktop app.** It shows in the notch while it answers. Booki only watches the size and date of one local file; it never opens it or reads your conversations.

## Settings

- What the notch shows, its hover delay and the Exceptions apps are now kept after a restart (they were lost before).
- The notch has its own section. The Dock page drops repeated options: glass opacity sits under Glass, presets are buttons beside the style; Reduce transparency, Compact, the native material switch and the taskbar timing details stay only for whoever had changed them.

## Reliability

- Buttons that appear on hover no longer cover text: clipboard, files in groups, the position picker.

## Not verified on a Windows PC

Tested in a browser with a simulated Windows bridge. A real PC still needs to confirm: the Claude app detection (a heuristic: opening an old chat may briefly count as working), the notch with a video on a second monitor, and that settings survive a restart.
