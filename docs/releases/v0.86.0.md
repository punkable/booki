# Booki 0.86.0

Booki feels more like Windows 11, a live notch widens into the dock, and new widgets show your devices and your AI usage.

## More Windows, smoother

- **Fluent throughout.** The context menu works like a WinUI menu (a quiet hover fill, 8 px panel, 4 px rows, 14 px text). Groups, widgets and Settings use Windows 11's accent palette; groups saved with the old colours show the matching Windows shade. Corners follow the 4/8 px rule, type sizes and motion follow Windows' steps, keyboard focus has one ring, scrollbars are thin, and dock hints behave like the taskbar's (a deliberate first hover, near-instant when sliding to the next tile).
- **The notch becomes the dock.** With something playing or a timer running, the dock grows out of the capsule where the notch really sits: first sideways to full width, then to its height, with one spring for the whole move.
- **No more blinking.** Booki no longer drops the dock and the notch out of "always on top" every three seconds, shows the dock before hiding the notch, and lets the CSS blur take over at once when the native blur steps aside.

## Widgets and plugins

- **Devices.** Battery of Bluetooth headphones, mice and keyboards (the value Windows shows in Settings), Logitech devices on a Bolt or Unifying receiver through Logitech's HID++ 2.0 protocol, and wireless headsets through HeadsetControl. The card shows the device closest to running out; the hint lists them all.
- **AI usage (opt-in).** Your Claude 5-hour and weekly limits and ChatGPT/Codex windows. Booki reads the sign-in Claude Code and Codex already keep on your PC and sends each only to its own service; it never stores, logs or forwards it, and asks every five minutes only while the widget is on the dock. These are the services' own app endpoints, so a change on their side can break the widget until Booki is updated.
- **Plugins.** Settings > System > Plugins installs optional open-source tools. HeadsetControl (GPL-3.0) is the first: Booki downloads the pinned 4.1.0 release from GitHub, checks it against GitHub's published SHA-256 digest, keeps it in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Booki\plugins`, and runs it as a separate program.
- **Charging.** Plugging the charger in shows a short "Charging" moment on the notch, and a battery at 10% or less outranks the music.

## Easier

- **Start with Windows, quietly.** The dock appears at once while Booki runs at below-normal priority for its first minute and defers non-urgent work until sign-in settles.
- **A first dock that looks like yours**, filled from your most-used apps, a clock and the trash.
- **Clearer Settings.** The Dock page shows the essentials with the rest under More settings; Apps & folders reads top to bottom (where items go, the source, then search).
- **Cleanup.** Unused code and styles removed, eleven unclosed stylesheet comments repaired (they had silently switched off rules such as the Konami party mode), windows-rs 0.62, Vite 8 and TypeScript 7.

## Verification limits

Automated browser tests use a simulated Windows bridge, and the Windows CI builds and packages the app. Device batteries (Bluetooth, Logitech receivers, HeadsetControl), the AI usage endpoints, the start-at-sign-in priority and the notch-to-dock motion need to be checked on a Windows 11 device.
