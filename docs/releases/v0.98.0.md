# Booki 0.98.0

Clearer Settings, like Windows 11: each widget and dock item gets its own page, colours use Booki's palette, and the dock no longer blocks clicks on half the screen.

## Settings

- **Pages instead of squeezed panels.** Choosing a widget or a dock item opens its own page, titled like Windows Settings (Widgets › AI usage, Apps & folders › Work). The parent is a link back, Escape goes back too, and focus returns to the item. No narrow side column at any window size.
- **Apps & folders.** A command bar with **New** (group, separator, Settings, Recycle Bin) and **Add apps**. An item's page shows its settings as cards: name, appearance, contents, icon and location; moving, ungrouping and removing live in its ··· menu. A group's page shows its apps to reorder or take out. Apps, folders and sites are added from a dialog: a click selects, + adds at once, several can be added together.
- **Colours like Windows Personalization.** Background and accent colour each show a preview, what is in use and a drop-down for the source. The background is Automatic, From wallpaper or one of Booki's own dark and light fills; the free colour picker and the accent-filled dock are gone from it (a saved accent fill becomes Automatic).
- The widgets on your dock wrap into a grid instead of scrolling sideways.

## Fixes

- **Clicks pass through the dock again.** After the dock was shown again, its whole transparent window kept capturing clicks, so programs on the lower half of the screen could not be moved or used until the pointer crossed the bar.
- **Why Claude's usage does not show.** Under Real Claude plan usage, Booki says whether Claude Code has called it and whether it sent your plan usage (only on Pro or Max plans, after the session's first reply).

## Not verified on a Windows PC

The new pages were checked in a browser at several window sizes. The click-through fix and the Claude status line still need confirming on a real PC.
