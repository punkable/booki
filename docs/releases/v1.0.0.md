# Booki 1.0.0

Your dock, finally alive. Groups that work as a widget board, apps that show what they're doing and a dynamic notch in every mode.

## Widgets and apps

- **Widget board in groups.** A group with widgets opens like the Windows Widgets board. Cards are small, medium or wide (right-click a card) and sit among apps in the order you drag them; the rest glide aside while you move one. Read-outs grow to fill their card.
- **App cards.** Right-click a pinned app › **Show as widget**. A music player shows the song and its controls (only for its own music); a browser shows how many windows are open, the active tab and buttons for a new or private window.
- **Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth and Settings widgets.** Your network and signal with the networks nearby (Windows' own list does the connecting), a Bluetooth switch, and shortcuts to the Windows Settings pages you use most.
- **Device notices.** With the Devices widget on the dock, headphones or another device connecting or disconnecting shows a notice.

## Behaviour and notch

- **Behaviour, rethought.** Illustrated cards answer three questions: when the dock hides, how it comes back and what stays while it's hidden. Choosing one no longer silently changes another. Pointing at the notch waits 0.3 s (adjustable) so brushing past does not open it.
- **Exceptions.** Hide in fullscreen, plus a list of apps the dock always steps aside for.
- **Dynamic notch everywhere.** Music, focus timer, battery, volume and AI agents now show with the hover trigger too; you pick what the notch shows, and over a maximized app it still shows what's urgent.

## Settings

- A new **About** tab holds what's new, help, links and support.
- Hover no longer draws a second border around widgets and groups; ring icons stay clear of their number; the widget catalog uses lighter buttons.

## Reliability

- A stalled Windows media service can no longer freeze the widgets: each query has a 3 s limit and play/pause never waits behind a poll.
- Fixed: a player card controlling another app's music; the exception list applying only after a window switch; a ghost label behind an open group; grouped widgets collapsing with the dock on a side edge.

## Not verified on a Windows PC

Automated tests run the interface in a browser with a simulated Windows bridge. A real PC still needs to confirm: Wi‑Fi readings from `netsh` in different Windows languages, the Bluetooth switch, per-app exceptions following the foreground window, app cards with real players and browsers, dragging inside groups and the notch timing.
