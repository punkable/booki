# Booki 0.88.1

## Motion
- The dock slides into its edge and back out like the Windows taskbar. Leaving accelerates in 167 ms and arriving decelerates in 250 ms. It is never clipped to a capsule, squashed, skewed or blurred. This replaces the notch-to-dock morph and the genie.
- The notch fades in where it sits instead of popping from 78 %.
- Music opens the notch for five seconds when it starts or the track changes, then lets it settle.
- The floating pill and notch notices use a tight shadow that fits their window, so no cut band shows below them.

## Panels and tooltips
- **Closing:** every widget panel closes the same way as a folder. That means a click outside, Escape, a second click on its tile, or switching to another app. Only one flyout is open at a time.
- **Look:** widget panels and the note pad share the folder flyout's material, outline, shadow, entrance and exit.
- **Tooltips:** dock hints are Windows tooltips. They show the name on one small line. A second line appears only for text the tile cuts off, or for several windows to pick from.
- **Plugin offers in panels:** the offer says what the plugin adds, then shows the terms box and the button side by side.
- **Theme:** reading cards follow the theme.
- **Settings in a group:** Booki's Settings pin inside a group shows as Settings with its gear, not as a folder named "Booki".

## Plugins
- **Large downloads:** a plugin download had a one-minute cap on the whole transfer, so FFmpeg (115 MB), rclone and yt-dlp failed on slower links. They now take as long as the connection needs. The Collector's direct-file downloads had the same cap.
- **Collector:** it shows download progress again, because yt-dlp's `--print` had hidden it. Titles and user folders with accents are no longer garbled.
- **Cloud:** reading the rclone status no longer fails on a malformed argument.
- **Older installs:** a yt-dlp installed before FFmpeg and gallery-dl were added offers to complete itself.
- **Updates:** the winget list accepts source agreements on its first run.
- **Settings > Plugins:** the page is now a grid of compact cards. The shared notes and the terms acceptance appear once, at the top.

## Settings
- Saving a setting touches the windows and rewrites the clipboard file only when a setting they depend on changed. Before, this happened on every toggle, which made Settings and the dock stall.

The browser tests cover these flows. Motion, plugin downloads and window behaviour still need a physical Windows PC.
