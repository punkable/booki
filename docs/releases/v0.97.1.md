# Booki 0.97.1

Fixes the expanded dock blocking clicks on the lower half of the screen.

## Fixes

- **Clicks pass through the dock again.** After the dock was shown again (from the notch, a settings change or a second launch), its whole transparent window kept capturing clicks. Programs on the lower half of the screen could not be moved or used until the pointer crossed the bar. Every reveal now makes the window click-through again outside the bar and its open panels.
- **Why Claude's usage does not show.** Under **Real Claude plan usage** in the AI usage widget, Booki now says whether Claude Code has called it, and whether that call carried your plan usage. Claude Code only sends it on Pro or Max plans, after the session's first reply.

## Not verified on a Windows PC

The click-through fix follows the cursor watcher's logic and was checked against the dock's reported hit areas in a browser; it still needs confirming on a real PC.
