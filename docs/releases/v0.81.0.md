# Booki 0.81.0

Redesigned Settings, a notch Island that actually informs, and groups with colour and icon.

- **Settings, redesigned.** Home shows your dock on a small desktop with an at-a-glance summary and what's new. Every page has coloured navigation, section icons and clearer hierarchy; the Dock page keeps a live stage preview at the top.
- **Apps & folders, rebuilt.** The dock being edited is a tidy grid of equal tiles with one toolbar (group, separator, Settings icon, Recycle Bin, undo). Widgets are arranged in the Widgets page instead. The library has tabs (Apps, Most used, Open now, Folders, Websites) with large icons; add and select appear on hover.
- **Groups with colour and icon.** A group can take a colour from a curated palette (eleven families, three shades each) and an icon from the library. Both are compact pickers that open a popover, and they show in the dock, the group flyout and the Settings preview.
- **Notch Island.** A third notch style: a small dot that opens into a capsule for a running focus timer (with a progress ring), playing music or low battery, and into a card on hover.

## Verification limits

Automated browser tests use a simulated Windows bridge. The Island notch geometry and group rendering still need to be checked on a Windows 11 device.
